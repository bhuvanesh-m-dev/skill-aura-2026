Prompt2Portfolio is a 45-minute AI-powered portfolio-building challenge conducted as part of SkillAura 2026. Participants are given a fictional student profile containing their education, skills, projects, certifications, achievements, and career goals, and must transform that information into a professional, responsive, internet-ready portfolio using any LLM or AI development tool.

The challenge focuses on prompt engineering, AI-assisted development, creativity, UI/UX, and rapid prototyping. Participants first configure their portfolio through an AI-guided questionnaire and then receive three Golden Lives—Create, Refine, and Signature—to progressively improve their portfolio. Prompt engineering is fundamentally about crafting and refining instructions to guide AI models toward desired outputs.

Every team leaves the challenge with a fully developed portfolio concept that demonstrates how AI can transform basic student information into a professional digital presence—something they can later apply to their own internship and placement portfolios.

The Challenge Details

Duration: 45 Minutes

Focus: Prompt Engineering, AI-Assisted Development, UI/UX Design, Creative Thinking, Rapid Prototyping, Web Development, Iterative Refinement, and Personal Branding.

The Three Golden Lives:

  • Create: Build the initial portfolio structure using an AI-guided questionnaire.
  • Refine: Iterate and improve your portfolio design, UX/UI, and content.
  • Signature: Add unique touches, personal branding elements, and creative flourishes.
